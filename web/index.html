Scope

A finding counts when it crosses a security boundary in a stock distribution configuration. Root-only and generic bugs do not, and the bar sits above what the CVE process applies: no CVE is requested below it, though one assigned to the patch commit is carried anyway. Every finding has a reproducible PoC.

Disclosure

Patched and in-process issues are opened; an unfixed one carries only its identifier and the fingerprint of its report. No PoC, environment or report is published yet. We are working on a responsible way to open them, similar to how syzbot discloses what it finds.

Patching

LLMs assist, but nothing is sent until the human who signs it off has understood the bug, validated the patch and tested the patched kernel. We follow the community's documented process, and condemn any use of this register to send LLM-generated patches no human has validated. Our thanks to the maintainers, reviewers and contributors who keep that process running.
